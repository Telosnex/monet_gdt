# Model card: monet_gdt

## Model

- **What it does:** from one wallpaper image, scores 10 mood words,
  picks a 3-color theme, and scores 29 decorative shapes.
- **Architecture:** PE-Core tiny (Meta, Apache-2.0), first 6 transformer
  blocks at 224 px, with small heads; distilled from a fine-tuned
  384 px teacher. One ONNX graph, 5.55 MB (8-bit weights).
- **Runtime:** 10-15 ms on one Apple-silicon CPU core (onnxruntime).
- **License:** MIT (code), Apache-2.0 (PE-Core weights); see NOTICE.

## Intended use

Theming an app (fonts, colors, decorative shapes) from a wallpaper the
user chose, on the user's device.

**Out of scope:** any decision about people, including judging a person
in a photo; content moderation; image classification or search. The
mood words describe the look of an image, not its subject.

## Training data

- About 75K images: CC0 / public-domain Megalith-10m photos (Flickr),
  CC0 photos chosen because they look like wallpapers, and synthetic
  images from MONET (generated by Apache-2.0 models). Per-source
  licenses: [LICENSES.txt](../LICENSES.txt).
- Labels: Jev (jev-1.14.0) answering fixed questions
  ([prompts/](../prompts)). For colors, it chooses among candidates
  extracted by libmonet.

## Evaluation

Agreement with the labels on a held-out split and on 1,951 real wallpapers the
model never trained on; see the README's Results and the
[design doc](ADR_train_monet_gdt.md), appendix B. Fonts picked from the
mood scores were also checked in blind A/Bs.

## Limitations

- Synthetic images are saturated and centered; they help on wallpapers
  but cost everyday photos a little mood accuracy.
