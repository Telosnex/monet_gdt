# ADR 001 — How to train a monet GDT model
Status: Accepted (2026-10-26). Amended 2026-10-30: colors (D12).

Reference implementation: libmonet_gdt (private research repository, not
reproducible). App contract: telosnex `docs/design/ADR_monet_gdt.md`
(private); the model's inputs and outputs are restated in R3.

## 1. Problem

The shipped model (`libmonet_gdt.onnx`, sha `dcdfc212a159`) gives theme
colors, 10 mood scores and 29 shape scores from the wallpaper pixels. It
trained on two corpora. About 37% of its mood labels (4,667 of 12,713) and
half of its shape labels (5,000 of 10,111) come from Wallhaven. Wallhaven
images have no clear license for training, and Unsplash Lite's terms
allow internal use only, so neither is trained on.

The research repo cannot rebuild the model. Its steps live in worklogs and
in more than 100 one-off scripts. Some outputs came from script copies
saved by md5. Paths, seeds and splits differ per step.

This ADR states the resources, the Jev calls and the training steps for one
build from CC0 and public-domain images, which a second person can repeat.

## 2. Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Source | Hardness |
|----|-------------|--------|----------|
| R1 | Every training image is CC0, public domain, or generated by an Apache-2.0 model, with its source and license recorded per image. | owner | hard |
| R10 | Heads do well on wallpapers as users set them: polished photos, art and AI-generated images. | owner | soft |
| R2 | One command rebuilds every artifact from the raw images and the stored Jev answers. | owner | hard |
| R3 | The output graph has the app contract: input `image` uint8 [h, w, 3 or 4]; outputs `scores` [1, 10], `feature` [1, 192], `colors` [3, 3], `shapes` [1, 29]. | platform | hard |
| R4 | Each head trains on the feature that the shipped graph computes. | owner | hard |
| R5 | The pick rules that won the A/Bs stay fixed: colors rule_v1 (D10; replaced by D12 on 2026-10-30), fonts band (0.5, 0.8), shapes echoes x (1 − domain) x strong tone. | owner | hard |
| R6 | Jev questions are byte-identical to the validated ones. | owner | hard |
| R7 | Every pretrained weight file and dataset has a recorded license. | owner | hard |
| R8 | Jev cost for a full build is less than $100. | assumed | soft |
| R9 | A full build runs on one Mac (M4 Max class) in less than one day. | assumed | soft |

## 3. Decisions

```
D1: The corpus is about 25,000 images, all from MONET (jasperai/monet
    v1.2.0), in three parts:
    - 1/2 real photos: `megalith10m` (D9).
    - 1/4 generic synthetic: `synthetic-flux-schnell`, `-flux-klein`,
      `-z-image`, chosen by the wallpaper prompts of
      experiments/synthetic_ab/select.py.
    - 1/4 genre synthetic: the same generators, chosen by the dark,
      game, anime and neon prompts of experiments/genre_ab/select.py.
    Each part is chosen by CLIP B/32 text score on MONET's stored CLIP
    embeddings, never by Unsplash or Wallhaven images (D8).
    Filters for every part: LAION aesthetic >= 5; wk_jasperai and wk_laion
    < 0.5; NSFW < 0.2; short side >= 512 px; aspect <= 3:1. Megalith also:
    no caption (any of MONET's 4) that names a watermark, logo, signature,
    copyright or overlaid text.
    Because: R1, R10. Mood A/Bs (ridge on the shipped feature, mean r vs
    Jev, train size fixed at 4,000), real only -> this mix:
    Wallhaven 0.650 -> 0.708, Unsplash 0.645 -> 0.653, everyday photos
    0.671 -> 0.649. Against real + generic synthetic, the genre quarter
    adds +0.020..+0.034 on Wallhaven at no cost on photos.
    Instead of: Wallhaven, Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay (no clear training
    license); MONET laion, coyo, cc12m, diffusion-aesthetic-4k (web
    scraped, no per-image license); CommonCatalog (CC BY); museum CC0 art
    (experiments/museum_ab: every mix with it lost 0.014..0.052 on
    Wallhaven and 0.023..0.099 on Unsplash; Jev rates it a separate
    "formal classical" domain); real only (worse on wallpapers);
    synthetic only (worse on photos).

D9: Megalith photos are used on the license Flickr showed at Megalith's
    crawl, with no per-photo recheck:
    - Megalith kept only four Flickr license kinds: CC0, Public Domain
      Mark, "No known copyright restrictions" (Flickr Commons) and United
      States Government Work. None of them requires attribution or
      limits commercial use, so no credit list is needed.
    - CC0 and the public-domain grants cannot be revoked. A later change
      of the Flickr label does not withdraw the grant made at upload, so
      the crawl-time license is the evidence that matters.
    - The remaining risk is an upload that was mislabeled from the start
      (the curator estimates 1-2%, judged by watermarks and text). The
      watermark and caption filters (D1) remove most of these.
    - The manifest records, per photo: the Flickr page URL (Megalith
      `url_source`), the image URL, the MONET row ID and the Megalith and
      MONET dataset revisions.
    Because: R1, R7
    Instead of: a Flickr API recheck (standard keys are non-commercial;
    a commercial key needs review); Openverse (cannot look up a given
    photo, its license data is its own crawl of the same metadata, and its
    terms forbid bulk scraping); scraping Flickr pages (the evidence would
    itself break Flickr's terms).

D8: Unsplash and Wallhaven images are never training data. The 1,951
    held-out labeled images from libmonet_gdt ("D8") scored the models of
    2026-10-25/26 (R10). Since 2026-10-26 D8 is optional: wallpaper
    likeness is judged with the Unsplash Lite reference (D11) and the
    blind A/Bs.
    Because: R1, R10
    Instead of: no wallpaper-like test: the CC0 test split has little art
    or game-style imagery.

D2: The split is fixed by sha256(photo ID): 90% train, 5% validation,
    5% test. Every step uses the same split.
    Because: R2
    Instead of: per-step random splits (the research repo): test images of
    one step trained another.

D3: Each image gets three Jev calls, all on jev-1.14.0. Prompts are files
    in `prompts/`, copied from the research repo:
    - mood: 10 choice questions, 5 levels, expected value (mood_fresh.ask).
    - colors: the production color request (P, S, T) over the app's
      options (D10), built by telosnex
      `dev/design/monet_gdt/color_requests_test.dart`; `prompts/colors.json` is
      the template. Labels are the per-role probabilities.
    - shapes: echoes_split_noul, 87 Noul questions over 29 shape images.
    Because: R6
    Instead of: one call with all questions: not validated. Extra
    questions changed answers before (fonts validity check).

D4: The test split gets a second run of each call, with a new shape order.
    This gives the Jev ceiling.
    Because: R2 (the report needs a ceiling)

D10: (Replaced in the graph by D12 on 2026-10-30.)
     Colors use rule_v1, chosen by blind A/Bs
     (experiments/color_prompt_ab):
     - Options: the <= 12 merged groups; each shows its most chromatic
       member with >= 1% of the group (vivid merge).
     - Request: each option labeled hex + HCT + share of the image (arm
       a2); the brief says what the labels mean.
     - Triad: Jev bounds each role (options with p >= 0.25 x the role's
       best); P = most chromatic; T = nearest P's complement hue among
       colorful options (HCT chroma >= 16, bands 30..180°), then most
       chromatic; S = colorful and >= 30° from P and T, then most
       chromatic, else 0.5 chroma + 0.5 hue gap from P. HCT rounded.
     - In the app: telosnex wallpaper_color_decisions.dart
       (libmonet monetGdtRuleV1Triad), parity 60/60 with rule_v1.py.
     - In the graph: the same rule over the graph's color areas (greedy
       ΔE 10 groups standing in for the merge), the student's per-role
       probabilities standing in for Jev's.
     Because: R5, R10. Votes: <=12 beat 255 (53-36); a2 beat hex only
     (22-16; 9-3 where it moved P to a larger area); rule_v1 beat Jev
     triads + chroma1_x10 (27-11); cutoff 0.25 vs 0.6 even (17-13).
     Instead of: chroma1_x10 on Jev's triad (lost 11-27); 32 or 255
     options (noisier, 1.4-5x tokens); swatch images (3x tokens, no
     clear gain); 3 sequential calls (tied with one call, 37-36); no
     hue rules in the brief (lost 11-26).

D5: Training order:
    1. Teacher: vit_pe_core_tiny_patch16_384.fb, last 4 of 12 blocks
       fine-tuned on mood (mood_unfreeze.py recipe).
    2. Student: 224 px, 6 blocks, distilled from the teacher on random
       crops (shrink_mood.py s224_d6_aug recipe, lr 1e-4, 20 epochs), on
       the train split plus the matched images (D11). The uncropped half
       of each batch takes its teacher targets from a cache (exact).
    3. Graph front end (onnx_graph.py): the color lists, and rule_v1 in
       place of chroma1_x10 (D10).
    4. Color model (hist_colors.py hist_img_conf_long, 30 epochs) on the
       front-end lists and the STUDENT feature.
    5. Shape head (train_shapes.py split, rank loss, winner weights) on
       the STUDENT feature.
    6. Graph build, w8, shape head appended (onnx_shape_head.py).
    7. App assets: mood map (export_app.py mood), reference fixtures.
    Because: R3, R4
    Instead of: heads on the teacher feature: the shape head lost 11
    points of top 3 when moved to the student feature.

D11: The student also trains on 50,000 Megalith photos that look like
    wallpapers (49,434 still on Flickr), chosen by "Unsplash likeness":
    1. Reference: Unsplash Lite (Hugging Face `1aurent/unsplash-lite`,
       25,000 photos; images from images.unsplash.com, long side 1024).
       Each reference photo -> CLIP B/32 (open_clip ViT-B-32, openai),
       L2-normalized (`match_unsplash.py ref`).
    2. Pool: every Megalith row in MONET that passes the D1 filters, with
       MONET's stored CLIP B/32 embedding; no downloads (`scan`). Local
       and stored embeddings agree (cosine median 0.991, `parity`).
    3. For each reference photo, its nearest pool photos by cosine,
       round-robin until N; one per SSCD cluster; none within cosine 0.95
       of a test image (`select`). Then download from Flickr (`download`).
    No Jev labels: the student only needs the teacher.
    Unsplash Lite license (Unsplash Dataset Terms, 2A, 3A-B, 3F): internal
    use to train models for internal business purposes; the photos and
    anything that discloses them (the reference embeddings,
    out/match/ref_clip.npy) are not redistributed, which is why they are
    not in this repository; and comparisons of the dataset with similar
    datasets are not published. The chosen Megalith list is CC0 data and
    is published.
    The 2026-10-25 build used libmonet_gdt's 11,217 non-held-out labeled
    images (Unsplash Lite + Wallhaven) as the reference; from 2026-10-26
    the reference is Unsplash Lite only.
    Because: R1, R10. D8: 0.776 vs 0.688 on the train split alone.
    Instead of: more Jev labels: +0.01 D8 per doubling vs +0.05 for
    student images (learning curves, appendix B).

D6: Font data (`font_scores.json`) is a separate build. It uses Google
    Fonts specimens, not wallpapers, so R1 does not apply. Copy its
    scripts; do not rerun it unless the font list changes.
    Because: R2, R8
    Instead of: rerunning it with every model build: same result, more
    cost.

D7: Jev answers are stored as raw JSONL, one line per call, keyed by photo
    ID and prompt sha256. Training reads only these files.
    Because: R2
    Instead of: derived label files: they hide which prompt made them.

D12 (2026-10-30): The graph's colors come from a head trained on the
    triads of monet_ui's MonetGdtRule20261030, not on Jev's color labels.
    - Labels: QuantizerCelebi, 128 clusters, on telosnex's 96 px
      thumbnail (integer box average to 384, then 96), then the rule.
      The rule chooses colorful colors before large areas. A neutral is
      never chosen while a colorful option with 1% of the image is
      unused. `tools/v2_labels` writes the labels for all 77,410 images
      (corpus and matched) in about 10 minutes.
    - Head: lib/hist_colors.HistNet, neigh=True. Each color in the
      graph's list gives CIELAB, chroma, share, and neighborhood share
      (the share of list colors within ΔE 10). The image feature is one
      more token. S is scored given P, and T given P and S.
    - Pick: argmax per role over the full list. S and T skip colors
      within ΔE 10 of an earlier pick.
    - Role order: S and T swap when S is the larger step from P (HCT
      hue if all three are colorful, else ΔE76), exactly as the rule.
      The graph reads HCT hue and "colorful" from two tables that
      monet_ui writes for the graph's 32,768 colors.
    - Steps (scripts/rebuild_graph.sh): build_graph.py builds a base
      graph with placeholder colors. scripts/v2_prep.py takes the
      feature, the color list and the thumbnail per image from it.
      tools/v2_labels writes the labels. Then scripts/train_colors_v2.py,
      then scripts/build_graph_v2.py, which replaces the base graph's
      color step. Scores, feature and shapes stay byte-identical to the
      base graph's, and to 2026.10.27's for the same student and shape
      head.
    - Test set: the matched holdout (4% of matched images by sha256 of
      the ID, 1,934 images). No step trains or tunes on it.
    Because: R10. Blind votes (appendix B2):
    - The first version of the rule beat the old on-device scorer 51 to 12.
    - A head with a speck mask beat the 2026.10.27 graph 34 to 8.
    - This head beat the head with the mask 35 to 15.
    Instead of:
    - Jev color labels + rule_v1: the rule's 12 options limit any head
      to ΔE 14 from the rule's triad. A pick from the full list can come
      within ΔE 1.8.
    - Roles scored independently, then rule_v1 (ΔE 29.2 vs 25.7).
    - The head without the neighborhood input, with a mask (S and T
      cover >= 0.7% of the image): lost 15 to 35.
    - The head's own S/T order: lost 13 to 32 against the rule's order.
    - No ΔE 10 exclusion: 8.6% of triads then hold two colors within
      ΔE 10 of each other.
    - The rule in Dart in the app: no visible gain (the head won 13 to
      6, 41 ties), and the graph is no longer complete for other users.
    - HCT inputs per color (sin and cos of hue, chroma, the rule's
      colorful flag), trained to 40 epochs, patience 8: ΔE 22.97 to the
      rule's triads (released 24.6), same Jev flags, and blind ties: 10
      to 19 on cards, 21 to 20 (19 ties) on the grid. Its S is about 3.5
      L* darker than the released head's. Closer to the rule no longer
      means better triads; a gain needs another target, such as votes.
```

## 4. Invariants

```
I1: The build from the same images and Jev files gives the same weights.
    If violated: a model change cannot be traced.
    Pinned by: build twice on the pilot set; compare sha256 (planned).

I2: No test-split image appears in any training or validation set.
    If violated: the reported scores are too high.
    Pinned by: a split check in every training step (planned).

I3: The graph's scores, feature and colors are bit-identical with and
    without the shape head.
    Pinned by: onnx_shape_head.py check (exists in libmonet_gdt).

I4: The app parity test passes on the new graph and assets.
    Pinned by: telosnex libmonet_gdt_parity_test.dart.

I5: The 2026.10.30 graph gives the same scores, feature and shapes as the
    2026.10.27 graph, and the same colors as the PyTorch color step.
    If violated: moods, fonts and shapes change without a test.
    Pinned by: scripts/build_graph_v2.py check (300 held-out images).

I6: The graph's role order equals MonetGdtRule20261030's role order.
    If violated: S and T swap against the rule on some images.
    Pinned by: tools/v2_labels/test/v2_order_test.dart on the 1,934
    held-out triads.
```

## 5. Formats & names

- Layout: `data/images/<source>/<id>.jpg`, `data/manifest.jsonl` (id, source, license, url,
  sha256 of the file, split), `jev/<call>.jsonl` (`mood`, `colors`,
  `shapes`, and `*_repeat` for D4), `prompts/`, `out/` (models, graph,
  reports). `data/` and `jev/` are never committed.
- Mood words, output order: artistic, attention-grabbing, calm, complex,
  disorderly, dramatic, formal, playful, strong, wide.
- Shape order: the graph metadata `libmonet_gdt.shapes`.
- `out/report.json`: per head, test metrics against Jev and the Jev
  ceiling; graph size and sha256.
- Colors (D12): `out/v2/prep_<shard>.npz`, `thumb96_<shard>.bin`,
  `labels_<shard>.jsonl` (`{i, triad: [P, S, T] hex}`),
  `labels_meta.json` (the rule file's sha256, the quantizer and the
  cluster count), `color_model.pt`, `monet_gdt_v2.onnx`, `check.json`.
- Version: the release date, Y.M.D without zero padding, `.N` for a second
  release on one day. ONNX `model_version` = YYYYMMDD x 100 + N.
  CHANGELOG.md lists each version.

## 6. Non-goals

```
NG1: New Jev questions or new pick rules.
     Reopens when: a blind A/B on test images beats a fixed rule.
     Reopened 2026-10-30 for colors (D12).
NG2: A new backbone or input size.
     Reopens when: the build is reproducible (I1) and a head is limited by
     the feature.
NG3: Training on Wallhaven, Unsplash or other corpora without a clear
     per-image license.
     Reopens when: a corpus with a clear training license exists.
```

## 7. Risks

1. A Megalith photo can carry a false license from its uploader (D9).
   The filters lower this but cannot prove it. If a rights holder
   objects, drop the photo by its Flickr ID and rebuild (R2).
2. The pretrained weights (PE-tiny for the model, DINOv3 for font shape
   data) have their own licenses, recorded in NOTICE (R7).
3. jev-1.14.0 can be retired. Then the stored answers are the only copy.
   Keep `jev/` backed up.
4. The color request and candidates come from app and libmonet code.
   A change there changes the color labels; the labels are pinned to
   those revisions. rule_v1 in the graph uses graph areas, not the merge; check
   its triads against the app's on the test split before shipping.
5. Synthetic images bias the heads toward their look (saturated,
   centered). The final mix costs everyday photos 0.022 of mood r.
   Recheck every test set in each build.
6. The A/Bs covered mood only. Colors and shapes are measured against
   the labels (appendix B). From 2026-10-30, blind votes also cover
   colors (D12).
7. The color labels depend on monet_ui code (D12). A change to
   MonetGdtRule20261030 or QuantizerCelebi changes the labels.
   labels_meta.json records the rule file's sha256. Keep the rule file
   unchanged, and give a changed rule a new dated name.
8. The 2026.10.30 head picks small color areas more often. Jev found a
   small speck color on 12 of 512 held-out images, against 0 for
   2026.10.27. The blind votes preferred the head (35 to 15) with this
   cost.

## 8. Workplan

Resources:
- Mac, Apple silicon, 32+ GB RAM, about 20 GB free disk.
- Python 3.12 venv from `pyproject.toml` + `uv.lock` (`uv sync`).
  Flutter for `tools/libmonet_tools` (HCT table and rule_v1 triads from
  libmonet; `scripts/libmonet_tools.sh`).
- Hugging Face access (MONET and Megalith parquet), `USER_API_KEY_JEV`.
  No Flickr or museum keys.

Jev calls, at the measured $0.042 per 1M input tokens:

| Call | Calls | Tokens per call | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| mood | 25,000 | 3.3K | $3.40 |
| colors | 25,000 | 3.3K | $3.50 |
| shapes | 25,000 | 34K | $36 |
| repeats (D4) | 3 x 1,250 | as above | about $2.10 |
| Total | | | about $45 |

Steps:
1. Create the repo layout (section 5). Copy the prompts and the needed
   scripts from libmonet_gdt. Record every license (R7).
2. Select and download the corpus with the D1 filters. Write the
   manifest with the D9 evidence and the split (D2).
3. Pilot: 200 images, all three calls. Measure tokens for colors. Jev
   rejects images with a short side under about 280 px; send at least
   320 px. Check colors and shapes heads on real vs synthetic (risk 6).
4. Run all Jev calls (D3, D4). Stop and report if more than 1% fail.
5. Train the teacher, then the student (D5 steps 1-2). Report test r and
   top 5% against Jev.
6. Build the front end. Train the color model and the shape head on the
   student feature (D5 steps 3-5).
7. Build the graph (D5 step 6). Run I3.
8. Write the app assets (D5 step 7). Run I1 on the pilot set.
9. Copy the graph and assets to telosnex. Run I4. Compare against the
   shipped model on the color, font and shape A/B pages.

### A. Alternatives & notes

- Keep the current model and relabel only: the Wallhaven images are still
  inside the trained weights (R1).
- One Jev call per image with all 107 questions: cheaper (the
  image is billed once), not validated (D3).
- Reference numbers from the shipped build: teacher test r 0.898; student
  r 0.733, top 5% 0.648; color validation Jev ΔE 24.10; shape head (student
  feature) top 1 33%, Jev top in top 3 56%. Wall time: student 21 min,
  color model 5 min, shape head under 1 min.

### B. Results (2026-10-26; the shipped build)

Mood vs Jev (r: mean Pearson over 10 words; top 5%: picked font in Jev's
top 5%). Test = new-corpus test split; D8 = held-out Unsplash/Wallhaven.

| Model | Test r | Test top 5% | D8 r | D8 top 5% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Teacher (25,230 labels) | 0.936 | 0.93 | 0.870 | 0.81 |
| Student, train split only | 0.831 | 0.78 | 0.688 | 0.55 |
| Student + matched (shipped) | 0.866 | 0.82 | 0.776 | 0.66 |
| Previous shipped student (libmonet_gdt) | | | 0.733 | 0.65 |

Graph: 5.55 MB (w8), 10-15 ms on one Apple-silicon CPU core; fp32 colors = PyTorch 297/300, w8 =
fp32 300/300; graph rule = libmonet rule 27,976/27,976. Colors: test ΔE
to the app triad 31.3 (ceiling with Jev's own answers 27.6). Shapes: top
1 0.361, Jev's top in top 3 0.61 (Jev vs itself 0.53 / 0.72).

Learning curves (fit 1 - r = a n^b; out/curves, out/learning_curve.json):

| Lever | Gap closed per doubling (test / D8) | D8 per doubling |
|---|---|---|
| Jev labels, fine-tuned teacher | 9.6% / 8.3% | +0.01 |
| Jev labels, frozen backbone | 11.4% / 8.4% | +0.01 |
| Student images (train split) | 19.5% / 13.1% | +0.05 |

Blind A/Bs: fonts, shipped vs new, 93 of 99 pairs "both fine".
Small r gaps (0.03-0.05) did not show in fonts.

Speed (M-series GPU, batch 64): teacher forward 56% of a student step;
caching the uncropped half 1.44x; bf16/fp16 no gain on MPS.

Dead ends: color model on student vs teacher features (same ΔE); no-crop
student (D8 0.657); reduced precision on MPS.

### B2. Results, colors (2026-10-30; D12)

ΔE is ΔE76 to the label triad, mean over P, S and T. Blind votes: the
owner, on cards that show P, S and T next to the wallpaper, with random
sides and no names. The first vote used 64 corpus images (40 to 3 on the
images where Jev found a missing colorful color). The other votes and the
Jev checks used matched-holdout images that no earlier vote or check used.

| Graph | ΔE to rule triad (matched holdout) |
|---|---|
| 2026.10.27 (Jev head + rule_v1) | 32.3 |
| Head scores roles independently, then rule_v1 | 29.2 |
| Head with S given P, T given P and S | 25.7 |
| Head with S given P, T given P and S, mask 0.7% | 24.2 |
| 2026.10.30 (neighborhood input, no mask) | 24.6 |
| HCT inputs, longer training (not shipped) | 22.97 |

| Blind vote (images where the triads differ) | Result | p |
|---|---|---|
| Rule, first version, vs old on-device scorer (ScorerTriad) | 51 to 12 | < 0.001 |
| Head + mask vs 2026.10.27 | 34 to 8 | < 0.001 |
| Rule's S/T order vs the head's order | 32 to 13 | 0.007 |
| Head + mask vs the rule in Dart | 13 to 6, 41 ties | 0.17 |
| 2026.10.30 vs head + mask (two sessions) | 35 to 15 | 0.007 |
| HCT inputs vs 2026.10.30, cards | 10 to 19, 16 ties | 0.14 |
| HCT inputs vs 2026.10.30, grid | 21 to 20, 19 ties | 1.0 |

On cards, the swatches are large, and the owner judged them as colors on
their own. The grid shows the two triads in columns on either side of the
wallpaper. Use the grid (`tools/vote.py`, `/grid?blind=1`) for color
votes. The HCT vote was decided on the grid. The earlier votes used
cards. The vote logs were not kept; the counts here are the record.

Jev checks on 512 held-out images (flag at p >= 0.9):

| Check | 2026.10.27 | Head + mask | 2026.10.30 | HCT inputs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Missing colorful color, with a dull swatch | 43 | 7 | 3 | 3 |
| Small speck color | 0 | 1 | 12 | 12 |
| Same hue twice | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 |

The HCT column is a fresh run on the same 512 images. In it, 2026.10.30
had 14 specks (p = 0.75 for the difference).

Graph: 5.55 MB (+4 KB). Median per image, desktop Mac CPU: 15.3 ms vs
14.6 ms on 1 thread, 9.7 ms vs 8.8 ms on 4 threads. The extra time is the
neighborhood share (n x n distances, n <= 2,048). The fp16 head changes
4 of 1,934 held-out triads against fp32 (3 shifts of ΔE 2.8-9.0, 1 other
T).
