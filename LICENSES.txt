TRAINING IMAGES
- Megalith-10m (madebyollin/megalith-10m @ dcdd5dc4381e), via MONET
  megalith10m: Flickr photos whose license at crawl was CC0, Public Domain
  Mark, "No known copyright restrictions" (Flickr Commons) or United States
  Government Work. None requires attribution or limits commercial use.
  Reasoning and evidence kept per photo: ADR D9.
  Two sets: the labeled corpus (14,400, MONET's 1024 px copies) and the
  student-only matched set (49,434 of 50,000 chosen; same source, same
  licenses), downloaded from each photo's Flickr URL in MONET and stored
  at 448 px short side. 566 were gone from Flickr.
- MONET synthetic (jasperai/monet @ a718d526ddcd, v1.2.0):
  synthetic-flux-schnell  FLUX.1-schnell    Apache-2.0
  synthetic-flux-klein    FLUX.2-klein-4B   Apache-2.0 (the 9B is
                                            non-commercial; MONET's card
                                            names the 4B)
  synthetic-z-image       Z-Image           Apache-2.0
  MONET card: "generated with Apache-2.0 models, whose outputs are
  redistributable". MONET packaging: Apache-2.0.

UNSPLASH / WALLHAVEN IMAGES (never trained on; not in this repository)
- Test only: 1,951 labeled images from a private research corpus score
  models (scripts/score_d8.py and others; ADR D8).
- Choosing training images (ADR D11): CLIP B/32 embeddings of Unsplash
  Lite photos picked which CC0 Megalith photos to add
  (scripts/match_unsplash.py). Their pixels are not trained on and
  nothing derived from them ships; only the choice of Megalith photos
  depends on them.
- Unsplash Lite (Hugging Face 1aurent/unsplash-lite; Unsplash Dataset
  Terms): licensed to download and store, and to use internally to train
  models for internal business purposes (2A); not to be disclosed,
  published or redistributed (3A, 3B). Hence the photos, their URLs and
  the reference embeddings are not in this repository.

- PE-Core tiny (timm/vit_pe_core_tiny_patch16_384.fb @ 29e4ebb6cc;

- CLIP ViT-B/32 (OpenAI, via open_clip): MIT (OpenAI CLIP repository).

  military / ITAR use; acknowledge DINOv3 in publications. Used only to

FONTS
- fonts/mood_font_model.json: names of 410 Google Fonts families and a
  10-number mood vector per family (Jev's answers about rendered
  specimens). No font files. Google Fonts families are under the SIL Open
  Font License 1.1 (a few Apache-2.0 or Ubuntu Font License); names are
  used only to identify the families. Its wall_population field (mood
  values of 272 Pixel, Wallhaven and Unsplash Lite wallpapers) is an
  earlier calibration, not used by the build (export_app.py
  WALL_POP=legacy).
- fonts/wall_population.json: the font calibration: Jev's mood values
  for 1,500 CC0 / public-domain Megalith photos.
